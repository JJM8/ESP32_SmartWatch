A fully open-source, 3D-printable wrist computer running a customizable agentic firmware. Designed for developers, privacy advocates, and the future of wearable computing.

Wrist-mounted Intelligence.

More than a notifier. The AgenticSmartwatch runs a local LLM with full tool access. Query your system, control your environment, and execute tasks via voice or text, all from a 3D-printed enclosure on your wrist.

Powered by the ILI9341 display driver and optimized for extreme battery life, it bridges the gap between wearable hardware and agentic software.

Agentic Capabilities
01

Local Inference

Runs quantized LLMs directly on the device. No cloud dependency. Your voice and data never leave the watch.

02

Voice Interface

Always-ready microphone with wake-word detection. Speak naturally to execute complex commands or queries.

03

Tool Execution

Connects to your local machine to run terminal commands, manage files, and control smart home devices.

Custom Agents

Write custom Python agents and drop them into the firmware. Extend functionality without recompiling.

Advanced power management ensures days of battery life, waking instantly for voice or touch interaction.
